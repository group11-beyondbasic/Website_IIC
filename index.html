TIPS FOR SPEAKING

Small habits that help you speak English more confidently. Start with one tip this week.

01

Speak every day, even for 10 minutes

Consistency matters more than length. Pick one topic each day and talk about it out loud for 10 minutes, alone or with a friend.

02

Learn phrases, not single words

Memorize chunks like 'I see your point, although...' or 'Would you mind...?'. They come out faster in real conversation than isolated vocabulary.

03

Shadow a native speaker

Play a short clip, then repeat it right after the speaker, copying rhythm, stress, and intonation. Stop and replay whenever you lose the pace.

04

Record yourself and listen back

Use your phone to record a 1-minute answer. Listen once for pronunciation and once for grammar. Most people notice their habits this way for the first time.

05

Think in English for a few minutes

Describe your room, your meal, or your plans in English in your head. This builds the habit of thinking in English before you need to speak.

06

Use fillers and bridge phrases

Words like 'Well,' 'Actually,' or 'Let me think...' buy you time. Silence feels longer to you than it does to the listener.

07

Ask when you do not understand

'Could you say that again?' and 'Do you mean...?' are normal in English conversation. Asking keeps the talk going instead of stopping it.

08

Focus on being understood, not perfect

Clear stress on key words matters more than perfect grammar. Slow down a little, and pause between ideas so the listener can follow.

My Experience with AI Apps

Notes after two weeks of practice with three apps.

SMALLTALK2ME

Used for: Speaking tests and structured practice.

I recorded my answers and the app gave feedback on pronunciation, fluency, grammar and vocabulary. The job interview and IELTS-style practice made me feel ready for real tests.

Good: Clear feedback after every answer, plus a level test.

Watch out: Some features need a paid plan, so check what is free.

Best for: Tests and interviews.

CHATGPT

Used for: Free conversation and role-play.

I asked it to act as a waiter, a teacher and an interviewer. With voice mode I could talk about any topic, and it explained my mistakes in simple words.

Good: Any topic, any level, detailed corrections.

Watch out: It can be too polite, so ask it to correct every mistake.

Best for: Flexible conversation.

PI (PI.AI)

Used for: Friendly everyday chat.

Pi feels like talking to a kind friend. It asks short questions and listens, so I relaxed and spoke longer. I chose a voice I liked and chatted while walking.

Good: Warm, natural, low pressure.

Watch out: It corrects grammar less than the others, so ask for corrections.

Best for: Confidence and small talk.

WHAT I LEARNED

Each app has a different job. Smalltalk2Me tests me, ChatGPT teaches me, and Pi helps me relax and keep talking. The progress came from speaking out loud every day.

Tutorials

Tutorial 1: Smalltalk2Me (speaking practice with feedback)

  1. Go to smalltalk2.me in your browser and create a free account.
  2. Choose a practice type, such as the level test, a job interview, or an IELTS speaking simulator.
  3. Allow the microphone when your browser asks, and sit in a quiet place.
  4. Read the question, then press record and answer out loud.
  5. Read the feedback on pronunciation, fluency, grammar and vocabulary, then try the same question again.

Tutorial 2: ChatGPT (conversation and role-play)

  1. Open chatgpt.com or the ChatGPT app and sign in.
  2. Copy the prompt below and paste it into a new chat.
  3. Tap the microphone or voice icon to speak, or type your answers.
  4. Read each correction, then say the corrected sentence out loud.
  5. At the end, ask: "Give me a summary of my five most common mistakes."

Tutorial 3: Pi (relaxed everyday chat)

  1. Go to pi.ai or open the Pi app and start a chat.
  2. Choose a voice you like in the voice settings and tap the voice or call button.
  3. Start simple: "Hi Pi, let's talk about my day."
  4. Keep talking for five minutes, even if you make mistakes.
  5. Finish by saying: "Please tell me three mistakes I made and how to fix them."

Prompt to copy (ChatGPT)

You are my friendly English speaking partner. My level is intermediate. Talk with me about everyday topics, one short question at a time. After each of my answers, correct my mistakes, show the corrected sentence, and explain the rule in one line. Then ask a follow-up question.
